Modding rules About the game

Horror you stand inside

Gilla is a VR horror game. There is no screen between you and the dark: you put the headset on, and you are in it.

Made with Claude

Gilla was made with heavy use of Claude, the AI model from Anthropic. A big part of the game came together through that work.

Mod Gilla however you want. Even in public lobbies.

The rule is simple. Every type of modding is allowed, and that goes for public lobbies as well as private ones.
